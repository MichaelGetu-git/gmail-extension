How opens are counted

An open is counted when the recipient's mail app loads the Zemenay logo in the footer. Each email's logo carries its own code, so every open is tied to one contact. Your own views don't count: not while you write the email, and not when you look at it in Sent.

Treat the rate as an estimate. Apple Mail loads every image on arrival, so its users show as opened whether they read it or not. Outlook and many company inboxes block images, so some real reads never show up. It's reliable for comparing templates and spotting a domain going to spam. Replies remain the number to trust.
